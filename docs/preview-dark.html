Welcome to Primary

Primary is soft, chewy, comforting — like a chocolate chip cookie, or a warm brownie. It merges play with work, and it was ported here from the Obsidian theme of the same name.

Fine-tuned to pixel and colour perfection, Primary aims to take away the need to think about customization, so that you can focus on what matters — the concepts, that link, this great idea. Underlined text stays quiet, struck-through text steps back, and inline code wears a warm little chip. Highlights come in three flavours: plain, bold and italic.

Read more at the Primary documentation, jump to Callouts, or mail hello@example.com.

中文排版

中文字形来自 HarmonyOS Sans SC，而拉丁字母、数字与标点仍由 Inter 渲染——Inter 覆盖不到的字符会自动回退到中文黑体字面，两者基线一致，混排不会跳字。这四个字体都已随主题打包成 woff2 放在 primary-fonts/，换一台没有安装这些字体的电脑打开，排版也不会变。

正文里的加粗中文、倾斜中文、行内中文代码都跟随同一套配色：加粗偏陶土红，斜体偏石板蓝。行距保持 Primary 原有的松弛感，段落之间留白充裕，长段落阅读不吃力。

代码块中的中文则由 Maple Mono NF CN 承担，等宽网格严格 2:1，中英文混排时纵向严格对齐：

配置项      说明                    默认值
theme.name  主题名称                Primary
theme.mode  明暗模式 light / dark   light
font.cjk    中文字体                HarmonyOS Sans SC
font.mono   代码字体                Maple Mono NF CN

提示类告示块同样支持中文标题与正文，图标与配色不受文字语种影响。
